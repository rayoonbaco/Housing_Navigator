# Housing Navigator: proposed business

**Resident promise:** Understand the housing rules returned for your home, in everyday language, and inspect the evidence behind each answer.

**First customer hypothesis:** Housing counseling organizations and housing providers that repeatedly explain rental requirements across several jurisdictions. Residents use the simple answer screen; staff inspect the evidence and unresolved facts.

**Proposed revenue:** An organization subscription for an address portfolio, evidence histories and reviewed change notifications. Pricing, willingness to pay and procurement requirements have not been validated. The hackathon prototype has no paying customers or demonstrated revenue.

**Why this could matter:** The same rule can turn on location, building facts, exemptions and dates. The product keeps those conditions visible, and distinguishes a missing fact from a missing legal review. A concise explanation links to the supporting rule, passage, date and source rather than asking a resident to trust an unsupported summary.

**What this demonstration proves:** A pipeline and inspectable saved results across the supplied challenge addresses. Exact performance and limitations are recorded in the release evidence. It does not prove nationwide coverage, legally certified answers or continuously monitored updates.

**Next commercial test:** Interview five housing counselors and five property-management staff. Ask them to work through real anonymized questions using the prototype, record comprehension and time to reach a supported answer, then ask what evidence review and update workflow they would pay for. Pilot one jurisdiction with a qualified legal reviewer before expanding.

**Presentation line:** “The resident gets a readable answer. The organization gets the evidence trail and a clear record of what still needs checking.”

The resident experience remains central. Organizational payment is a distribution and maintenance hypothesis, not a reason to hide uncertainty or skew answers toward the payer.
