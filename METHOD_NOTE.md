# Housing Navigator - method and limits

**Purpose.** Housing Navigator helps a resident understand which rental rules the saved engine returned for a supplied home address. Six everyday questions lead to concise explanations and claim-specific Check my homework evidence. It is a saved-result prototype, not legal advice or continuous monitoring.

**Method.** Automated extraction selects pinned source-passage IDs; code copies their text exactly. Schema and evidence checks precede independent model review. Geographic resolution keeps postal city separate from matched legal geography. Reviewed coverage predicates evaluate true, false or unknown. Date gates distinguish current, future, pending and failed measures. Change cases reuse previously reviewed source-rule mappings and recompute address/date outcomes.

**Completed evidence.** The release contains 310 rules, 289 model-reviewed predicates and 21 unresolved scopes for 500 addresses at four dates. A qualified city-label bug had skipped 117 city records. Correcting it with unchanged rules and property facts increases default returned rule/address records from 33,284 to 40,564; an independent recomputation matches every result. Website transport reproduces all 162,256 four-date records exactly while reducing each snapshot from about 36 MB to 1 MB.

**Explain and verify.** Nineteen everyday rewrites passed recorded model review. A subsequent source audit withheld six for semantic repair; two longer faithful replacements were subsequently source-audited and admitted under an explicit expandable-detail policy. A final two-call repair returned four model-supported candidates. Coordinator and red-team source audits admitted two unchanged and withheld two for a missing definition and incomplete citation coverage. Seventeen are now published and two remain withheld. Positive model review and exact citations alone do not guarantee faithful meaning. Fingerprints bind each explanation to an unchanged rule, and every attached passage must match saved evidence. Original claims and review outcomes remain in the audit trail.

**Tests and change cases.** The integrated engine passed 72 offline tests; eight release/import checks passed. UI checks cover fixtures, actual claim evidence/fingerprints, adversarial uncertainty/security cases, 12,000 topic summaries and lossless transport round trips. Local HTTP resources were served successfully. Five internal change checks pass with limited scope: T2 has 88 potential notifications and zero confirmed eligible addresses; T4 describes hypothetical pending bills, not current protections. The initial public Render deployment passed actual desktop interaction checks. The new drawer-density patch and mobile layout await live verification.

**Limits and next steps.** Twenty legal cities remain unresolved. Required owner, occupancy, facility, tenancy and other property facts cannot be invented. County geography is not county-law completeness. The corpus inventories 87 sources and 54 usable text captures; source access and organizer byte-hash discrepancies remain explicit. Precedence is not inferred from city level or headline numbers. A narrowly sourced state/local relationship requires separate review. The organization-subscription business is a proposal with no validated pricing, demand or revenue. Final videos and both submission receipts are still pending.
