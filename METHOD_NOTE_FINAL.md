# Housing Navigator - method and limits
Ray Gomez | RealPage challenge | Submission release, October 4, 2026

## Purpose and workflow
A resident chooses a supplied address and date, reads six everyday housing questions, and opens Check my homework for the supporting citation, exact passage and uncertainty. The website serves saved results; it is not legal advice or continuous monitoring.

Automated extraction selects IDs from pinned source passages; code copies their text exactly. Schema and evidence gates precede independent model review. Address resolution separates postal city from matched legal geography. Coverage evaluates true, false or unknown; date gates separate current, future, pending and failed measures. Change cases reuse reviewed source-rule mappings.

## Evidence and safeguards
The saved release contains 310 rules, 289 model-reviewed predicates, 21 unresolved scopes and 500 addresses at four dates. A city-label fix recovered 117 previously skipped city records; independent recomputation matches the 40,564 default records. Lossless website transport reproduces all 162,256 four-date records.

Seventeen everyday explanations were released after additional source audit. Positive model review alone is insufficient: final candidates were withheld for missing definitions or incomplete citation coverage. Fingerprints bind each published explanation to an unchanged rule, and every attached passage must match saved evidence. Original candidates, failures, reviews and usage receipts remain preserved.

## Validation and change cases
Recorded checks include 72 offline engine tests, eight release/import checks, adversarial uncertainty/evidence tests, 12,000 topic summaries and transport round trips. Live desktop Chrome checks on October 4 covered address selection, unmatched search, fair housing, next-step guidance, citations, dates and dismissal. Mobile was not retested in the final pass; simulated renter audits are not real-person studies.

Five internal change checks pass within their limits. T2 has 88 potential local notifications and zero confirmed eligible addresses; missing property or actor facts remain unknown. T4 concerns hypothetical pending bills. Original change narratives describe a labelled review baseline, not necessarily the selected date. These checks do not establish legal completeness or accuracy.

## Remaining limits and next milestone
Twenty legal cities remain unresolved. Owner, occupancy, certificate, tenancy and facility facts cannot be invented. County geography does not establish county-law completeness; state/local precedence needs separate source review. The supplied inventory has 87 sources and 54 usable text captures; access gaps and organizer byte-hash discrepancies remain explicit. Separately pinned texts do not resolve original-source integrity.

English is demonstrated; validated Spanish and nationwide expansion are not. The next milestone is a legally reviewed housing-counselor pilot and real-user comprehension testing. Organization subscriptions are a commercial hypothesis, with no validated demand, pricing or revenue.
