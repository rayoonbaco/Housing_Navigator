# Three separate videos

HackOS screenshots specify a team introduction, product demo and technical walkthrough. Each must be at most 60 seconds. Aim for 50–55 seconds, leaving room for a title or closing frame. Confirm final requirements in the live form before uploading.

## Record the introduction now

Use your real room and a steady camera at eye level. Put a lamp or window in front of you, rather than behind you. Record clean speech first; background music can be added later if its use is authorized and it does not obscure your voice. Your family and interests belong in this video; no staged hardship is needed.

Suggested script:

“I’m Ray Gomez. I’m 59, unemployed, and a father of four. About a year ago, I started learning AI, and I’ve poured myself into it ever since.

I love archaeology and metal detecting: finding evidence, following a lead, and seeing something overlooked start to tell a story.

That’s how I approached Housing Navigator. Housing law should be readable by the people whose homes it affects. I’m building it so you can get a plain answer and check exactly where it came from.

I’m competing solo, with AI as my tool. I want to show my kids—and myself—that I can still build something that matters.”

## Product demo: record after the release is frozen

Show one supplied address, six concise topic cards, one expanded “Check my homework” citation, and one honest missing-information result. Finish with a change scenario. Use actual observed outcomes; do not narrate a proposed eligibility fix as completed.

## Technical walkthrough: record after verification

Show the five stages—extract, resolve, apply, explain and track—then one exact evidence passage, independent review outcome and a saved test receipt. Explain that tests check specific behavior, while unresolved facts and source gaps remain visible. End with the proposed organization subscription and the next validation step; avoid claiming traction or legal certification.

## Before submission

Check that each file opens, speech is audible, duration is within the limit and the recording shows the final deployed product. Confirm the GitHub and demo links work for a signed-out viewer. Save both submission receipts.
